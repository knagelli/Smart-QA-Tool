Should I trust AI-generated test cases?

Think of it as hiring a fast, tireless junior tester — without the weeks it takes to recruit, onboard, and train one. Every test case should be reviewed before you rely on it; Req2QA does the time-consuming first pass, then hands it to you to approve, correct, or tick off.
