Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your credentials are used once, in memory, to run a live-execution test, and are never stored or logged. Uploaded documents are used only to generate your report and aren't retained afterward — only the resulting report is kept, and only for 7 days. Analysis is powered by a leading AI model, used solely to generate your report and never to train anyone else's model. See our Security Overview and Privacy Policy for the full detail.

What does this cost?

Pricing is based on your test scope and how much live execution you need — not a flat subscription fee. Tell us what you're testing and we'll quote a fair price for it.

Do I need a developer or automation experience to use this?

No. Upload the documents you already have and follow the on-screen prompts — Req2QA handles the technical work of generating test coverage and, if you want it, running tests against your environment.

What happens to my uploaded documents?

They're used only to generate your report and are not retained afterward. Only the resulting report and workbook are kept, for up to 7 days, reachable only via your private link — shown to you right after the run completes. Closed the tab before saving it? Look it up again anytime in Run History using your access code.

Can Req2QA test our custom-built (not off-the-shelf) application?

Yes — Custom Application Mode reads a process-flow diagram and a short brief about your app, then confirms its understanding with you before generating anything.
