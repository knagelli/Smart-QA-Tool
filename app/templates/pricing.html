Pricing, in the open

We're confident in what Req2QA does, so here's exactly how it's priced — base allowance, per-case rates, and where each engagement's actual invoice comes from. No login wall, no "contact us for a number."

How to read this page: every tier's price below is a base (covering up to 25 test cases) plus a declining per-case rate once you go beyond that. Your actual invoice is driven entirely by how many test cases you generate and/or execute — these numbers are the real rate card, not an illustrative range, but they are not a quote for your specific engagement. Tell us your scope and we'll work out the exact number together.

Returning for execution on cases you already paid to generate

No double-billing, ever

If you generated test cases under Tier 1 this month and come back for execution under Tier 3 next month, you're not charged again for writing them — the Tier 3 fee covers running them against your environment and reporting results, nothing else. On paid engagements, we keep up to 5 of your recent kept test-case sets for up to 90 days so you can select from them directly for execution — see Saved Test Cases.
