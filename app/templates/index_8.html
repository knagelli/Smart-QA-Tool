Requirements to Test Coverage

Requirement validation, test scenario generation & traceability matrix.

Req2QA turns the requirements documents you already have into structured test coverage — automatically. Upload what you've got, and get back validated test scenarios and a full traceability matrix in under a minute. Optionally, Req2QA can then run those test cases live against your own sandbox/UAT environment and report the result with screenshots, or you can bring test cases you've already written and skip generation entirely.

1
Upload your requirements document (or a process-flow diagram, for a custom-built app).

3
Run it live (optional) — Req2QA executes selected test cases against your sandbox/UAT environment and reports PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED with screenshot evidence.

Questions, or need an access code for your team? Contact kalyan@req2qa.com.

About Req2QA

Req2QA is built and operated by Kalyan, an independent QA automation practitioner. It exists for teams who need real, structured test coverage but don't have a dedicated automation engineer on staff — upload what you already have, and Req2QA does the validation, generation, and (optionally) execution work a manual process would otherwise take days on.

Every execution run happens only against a sandbox/UAT environment you supply — never production — and your credentials are used once, in memory, and never stored. See our Security Overview for the full detail, or reach out directly at kalyan@req2qa.com.
